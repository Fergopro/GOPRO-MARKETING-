How GoProcures Works

You give us the requirement.
We handle the procurement work.

Our process is designed for companies that want procurement handled properly without managing suppliers, quotations, follow-ups and sourcing themselves.

01 · Intake

Tell us what you need

Talk to the AI, type a request, send a BOQ/RFQ or contact our team directly.

02 · Clarify

We close the gaps

Specifications, quantities, delivery location, timing and commercial requirements are clarified before sourcing.

03 · Source

Our agent works the market

The procurement office approaches suitable sources and works through availability and commercial terms.

04 · Compare

We compare properly

The goal is the best overall deal — not simply the lowest number on paper.

05 · Return

We come back to you

You'll receive the procurement result and next steps through your preferred channel.

06 · Execute

Procurement continues

Once an option is selected, the process can continue into ordering, logistics and delivery.

AI in front. Procurement experts behind it.

The AI is the front door. Your procurement office remains responsible for sourcing, negotiation and decisions.
